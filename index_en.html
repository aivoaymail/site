Marketing

Challenge: Continuous content creation and SEO optimization require extensive time and expertise.
⚙️

✅

Sales

❌
Challenge: Time-consuming proposal creation and poorly maintained CRM records lead to lost deals.
⚙️
AI Approach: AI draft proposals, maintains customer data, and provides accurate sales forecasts.
✅

Administration

❌
Challenge: Manual verification and data entry of invoices and documents cost time and cause errors.
⚙️

✅

Challenge: Valuable company knowledge is scattered across folders, emails, and minds, making it hard to find.
