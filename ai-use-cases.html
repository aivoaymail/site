Use Cases

AI Use Case Directory for SMEs

Discover practical examples and typical scenarios showing how artificial intelligence simplifies work processes in small and medium-sized enterprises today.

🔍
Purchasing
📦

Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

AI predicts future demand for raw materials and products based on historical order data and seasonal patterns.

Challenge: Excess inventory binds capital, while supply bottlenecks disrupt operations.
AI Approach: Intelligent forecasting models determine the optimal order time.
Benefit: Reduction of storage costs and decreased out-of-stock risk.
Purchasing
📊

Automated Price Comparison

AI-supported systems crawl, structure, and compare supplier quotes in real time.

Challenge: Manually compiling supplier quotes in different formats takes hours.
AI Approach: Large language models extract items from PDFs and display them side-by-side.
Benefit: Quick decision-making basis and direct identification of savings potential.
Purchasing
⚠️

Supplier Risk Management

Continuous data analysis monitors reliability and price trends of global suppliers.

Challenge: Late warnings about supplier defaults endanger production.
AI Approach: AI evaluates external news and internal delivery reliability data.
Benefit: Proactive switching to alternative suppliers before a bottleneck occurs.
HR / Personnel
👥

Applicant Screening (Pre-selection)

AI matches incoming resumes in a structured way with the job requirements.

Challenge: Manually reviewing hundreds of unstructured profiles binds HR resources.
AI Approach: Semantic matching of qualifications and work experience.
Benefit: HR receives a pre-sorted evaluation of the most suitable candidates.
HR / Personnel
🤖

Employee Onboarding Assistant

An internal chatbot answers new team members' questions about processes, systems, and guidelines.

Challenge: Repetitive questions burden experienced colleagues during onboarding.
AI Approach: A RAG system searches for answers in SOPs and internal guidelines.
Benefit: Higher satisfaction for new employees and relief for existing team members.
HR / Personnel
✍️

Job Description Optimization

Optimizing job descriptions to increase conversion rates and precisely target candidate groups.

Challenge: Imprecise or unattractive postings attract the wrong profiles.
AI Approach: Writing tools adjust tone and keywords to the target group.
Benefit: Qualitatively better applications and faster filling of open positions.
Marketing
📢

SEO & Content Creation

Generating quality content for websites, blogs, and social media based on target keywords.

Challenge: Regular content creation costs agency budgets and time.
AI Approach: Text AI creates customized drafts optimized for search engines.
Benefit: Drastically reduced production time for marketing content.
Marketing
🎯

Customer Behavior Segmentation

AI analyzes customer profiles and purchasing behavior to automatically form marketing segments.

Challenge: One-size-fits-all newsletters have poor open and click rates.
AI Approach: Pattern recognition divides the customer base into interest groups.
Benefit: Personalized campaigns with measurably higher sales.
Marketing
📈

Paid Ads Campaign Management

Optimizing advertisements on Google and social media through automatic bid and budget adjustment.

Challenge: Daily manual adjustments in the ad manager require a lot of expertise.
AI Approach: Machine learning algorithms distribute budgets to the best-performing ads.
Benefit: Lower cost per click and maximum efficiency of the advertising budget.
Sales
💰

AI-Powered Proposal Generation

Drafting complex proposals based on specifications, CRM data, and price lists.

Challenge: Sales reps spend hours drafting technical proposals.
AI Approach: The system pulls required information from CRM/ERP and drafts the proposal.
Benefit: Reduced response times to customer inquiries and faster closings.
Sales
⚡

Intelligent Lead Scoring

Prioritizing incoming inquiries by probability of closing and customer value.

Challenge: Too much time is spent on low-potential leads.
AI Approach: Evaluation of leads based on historical sales data.
Benefit: Sales focus on the most lucrative opportunities.
Sales
✉️

Sales Email Follow-Ups

Personalized and timed follow-up emails to potential clients for reactivation.

Challenge: Following up on proposals often gets lost in daily operations.
AI Approach: AI formulates tailored emails based on previous conversation history.
Benefit: Higher lead-to-customer conversion rate with minimal effort.
Administration
📁

Document & Invoice Extraction

Incoming invoices are read, amounts verified, and automatically posted to the ERP system.

Challenge: Manual verification and entry of invoices binds accounting resources.
AI Approach: Intelligent OCR extracts metadata, taxes, and IBANs accurately.
Benefit: Virtually error-free and extremely accelerated invoice processing.
Administration
📨

Email Inbox Pre-Sorting

Incoming mail to general info boxes is classified and assigned to the correct contact person.

Challenge: Daily manual distribution of incoming emails causes delays.
AI Approach: Text classification analyzes subject and content for intent.
Benefit: Fast response times and clear structure in the team mailbox.
Administration
📋

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Automatic generation of structured SOPs from informal notes or audio recordings.

Challenge: Important workflows are not documented because documentation is too tedious.
AI Approach: AI structures informal notes into precise, easy-to-understand step-by-step guides.
Benefit: Easily accessible company knowledge and fast training times.
